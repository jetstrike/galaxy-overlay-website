Galaxy Overlay Setup Guide

Complete step-by-step instructions for Once Upon A Galaxy players on Android

Why is this setup required?

This setup (Developer Options and Wireless Debugging via Shizuku) is specifically required to allow Galaxy Overlay to read Once Upon A Galaxy's live log files. Modern Android security strictly isolates each game inside a private sandbox. Shizuku creates an authorized on-device bridge that enables real-time odds tracking with zero root, no PC connection after initial setup, and complete local privacy.

2. First-Time Setup (One-Time Pairing)

~3 Minutes • One-Time Only

Pairing only needs to be completed once. Future launches take less than 15 seconds.

1

Enable Developer Options

Open your phone's Settings app > About phone.

Scroll down to find Build number and tap it 7 times consecutively. Enter your device lock screen PIN/password if prompted.

2

Enable Wireless Debugging

Make sure your phone is currently connected to Wi-Fi.

Go to Settings > System > Developer options.

Scroll down to the Debugging section and toggle Wireless debugging to ON. Check Always allow on this network and tap Allow.

3

Pair Shizuku

Open the Shizuku app and scroll down to the Start via Wireless Debugging section.

  • Tap Pairing, then verify Shizuku notifications are enabled.
  • Tap Developer options to return to the Wireless Debugging settings screen.
  • Tap the text Wireless debugging to open its full details, then tap Pair device with pairing code.
  • A 6-digit code will appear on screen. Pull down your notification shade, tap Enter pairing code on the Shizuku notification, enter the 6 digits, and submit.
You will see a notification confirming Pairing successful.
4

Start Shizuku Service

Switch back to the Shizuku app.

Under Start via Wireless Debugging, tap the Start button. A terminal window will execute for ~2 seconds, and the main screen will display Shizuku is running with a green indicator.

5

Launch Galaxy Overlay & Authorize

Open Galaxy Overlay from your app drawer or home screen.

  • If prompted, grant the Display over other apps permission so the floating HUD can render over the game.
  • When Shizuku prompts Allow Galaxy Overlay to access Shizuku?, tap Allow all the time.
  • The compact overlay HUD pill will appear on your screen.
6

Launch Once Upon A Galaxy

Open Once Upon A Galaxy. Tap the floating overlay pill anytime during a match to expand into the full companion HUD and view live stats, shop odds, and opponent boards.

< 15 Seconds

4. Frequently Asked Questions

Troubleshooting

Shizuku says "Wireless debugging is not connected"

Ensure your device is connected to an active Wi-Fi network. Android disables Wireless Debugging whenever Wi-Fi disconnects. Reconnect to Wi-Fi, toggle Wireless Debugging back on in Developer Options, and tap Start in Shizuku.

Shizuku says "Authorized applications: 0"

Open Galaxy Overlay; it requests permission automatically on startup. If you missed the prompt, open Shizuku > tap Authorized applications > toggle Galaxy Overlay to ON.

The floating HUD does not appear over the game

Go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Galaxy Overlay > Display over other apps and verify the toggle is enabled.

Do I have to pair every time I restart my phone?

No. Pairing is a one-time process. After restarting your phone, just connect to Wi-Fi, ensure Wireless Debugging is enabled, and tap Start in Shizuku.
